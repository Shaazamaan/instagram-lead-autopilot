# What the owner does before saying "start"

Create these accounts (free) and stay logged in to all of them in the browser Claude will use (the Claude desktop app's built-in browser or Chrome with the Claude extension). Claude does the rest and will ask you only when a screen needs your own click or password.

1. **Instagram**: a Professional account (Business or Creator) for the brand. Settings > Messages: allow message requests from everyone.
2. **Facebook**: a Facebook Page for the brand, linked to the Instagram account (Instagram > Settings > Accounts Center, or Page settings > Linked accounts).
3. **Meta for Developers**: developers.facebook.com, register as a developer with the same Facebook login, and a Business portfolio (business.facebook.com) that owns the Page.
4. **GitHub**: github.com account. Claude creates the repository from this template.
5. **Cloudflare**: dash.cloudflare.com account (free plan). Open Workers & Pages once so the workers.dev subdomain is created.
6. **Discord**: a server with a channel for lead alerts, and the Discord app on your phone with notifications on. Claude creates the webhook.
7. **Hugging Face**: huggingface.co account (for the voice model). Claude creates a read token.

Have ready: what the business does, who buys (types of customers), what you never want said (e.g. prices), the language for videos and DMs, and the language(s) you take calls in.

After setup, your only job: call each lead from the Discord alert, then tell Claude `lead <id>: <what happened>` within 24 hours.
